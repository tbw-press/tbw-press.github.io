About

The Reset

Twenty-five years of enterprise IT engineering vanished when an eye disease took my sight and ended my career. It took five years in the wastelands to burn away the noise, strip out the optical illusions, and re-evaluate what matters.

At 52 and profoundly blind, I see the corporate music industry’s bullshit more clearly than ever. Losing my eyes didn’t shut me up—it stripped away the visual crutches that blind everyone else.

Blinded Warrior is the hard-fought result of that transition: a studio engineered on bare-metal math, zero visual distractions, and a zero-tolerance policy for fake perfection. I would rather be a thistle on a golf course than just another blade of grass.

The Weaponized Advantage

Modern audio engineering is broken. Studios spend thousands on glowing digital VU meters, fake vintage faceplates, and visual analyzers—teaching engineers to mix with their eyes instead of their ears.

When sight was removed from the equation, it didn’t break the workflow; it created a tactical advantage. Blinded Warrior operates on 100% blind, zero-GUI, ear-driven audio processing.

  • No GUI Bloat: Built exclusively on zero-GUI, 64-bit C++ processing (Airwindows) inside a custom Reaper/OSARA architecture. No eye candy. Pure signal math.
  • Pure Acoustic Truth: No waveforms or frequency graphs. Just phase alignment, transient impact, and harmonic glue in their rawest, most honest form.

The Code of Rebellion

Music was never meant to be sterile, grid-aligned, or pitch-corrected into oblivion. Blinded Warrior exists to serve the raw, heavy genres that still have blood on the floor: Hard Rock, Alt-Rock, Metal, Punk, and Grunge.

  • Zero Autotune: Flaws equal humanity. We mix for emotional danger, not plastic perfection.
  • Zero Quantizing: No snapping performances to a digital grid. Human timing variations create natural groove and explosive energy.
  • The Underground Offensive: Independent underground bands with something real to say get mixed and mastered completely free. This is an offensive against corporate paywalls, predatory studio rates, and assembly-line AI slop.

You get high-end, uncompromising audio engineering; the industry gets a middle finger. Art and creativity belong in our hands.
