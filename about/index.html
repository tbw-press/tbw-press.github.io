About

To my brothers and sisters who still believe that music is the voice of cultural and social change:

The Refocus

Imagine a 25-year IT engineering career vanishing, taken by an eye disease that steals your sight. It’s real.

After losing my career, it took five years in the wastelands to burn away the noise, strip out the optical illusions, and re-evaluate what matters.

At 52 and profoundly blind, I now see the corporate hypocrisy more clearly than ever. Losing my eyes didn’t shut me up; it stripped away all the visual crutches that blind the herd.

Blinded Warrior is the hard-fought result of that transition: an audio passion built on grit, zero visual distractions, and a zero-tolerance policy for fake perfection and AI slop. I would rather be a weed on a golf course than another blade of grass.

The Weaponized Advantage

Modern audio engineering is broken. Studios spend thousands on glowing digital meters, fake vintage faceplates, and visual analyzers — tech designed to strip the human out of the music.

When sight was removed from the equation, it didn’t break the workflow; it created a tactical advantage. Blinded Warrior operates on 100% ear-driven audio processing.

  • No Generic Bloat: Built exclusively on purposeful audio processing inside a custom Reaper architecture. No eye candy. Pure signal math.
  • Pure Acoustic Truth: No waveforms or frequency graphs. Just phase alignment, transient impact, and harmonic glue in their rawest, most honest form.

The Code of Rebellion

Music was never meant to be sterile, grid-aligned, or pitch-corrected into oblivion. Blinded Warrior exists to mix and master the raw, human, heavy genres that still have blood on the floor and a message of truth to shout into the world.

  • Zero Autotune: Flaws equal humanity. I believe in emotional truth, impact, and danger, not plastic perfection.
  • Zero Quantizing: No snapping performances to a digital grid. Human timing variations create natural groove and explosive energy.
  • The Underground Offensive: Independent and underground bands with something real to say get ears and a mixing rig completely for free. This is an offensive against corporate paywalls, predatory studio rates, and assembly-line AI slop.

You get my passion and desire to contribute substance; the industry, and corporate culture, get a middle finger. Art and creativity belong in OUR hands.

I do this 100% for free with no contracts, fees, invoices or commitments. I would love to discover new artists and see what I can do with their tracks.
